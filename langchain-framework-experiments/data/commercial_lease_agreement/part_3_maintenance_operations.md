# Commercial Lease Agreement - Part 3: Maintenance, Alterations & Operational Rules

- **Source Document ID:** `commercial_lease_agreement_part_3_maintenance_operations`
- **Document Version:** 2.1 (Expanded)
- **Date of Generation:** 2025-12-15

---

## 11. TENANT RESPONSIBILITIES

**11.1. Standards of Care**
Tenant shall, at its sole cost and expense, maintain the Premises and every part thereof in good, clean, and sanitary order and condition (fair wear and tear excepted). This obligation includes:

- **Interior Walls & Ceilings:** Maintaining paintwork and finishes; repairing any damage caused by Tenant's installations.
- **Flooring:** Regular cleaning and maintenance of carpets, tiles, or other floor coverings installed by Tenant.
- **Electrical:** Replacement of light bulbs, tubes, and starters within the Premises.
- **Pest Control:** Engaging a licensed pest control vendor to treat the Premises on a monthly basis.
- **Furniture & Fixtures:** Maintaining all Tenant-owned furniture, trade fixtures, and equipment.

**11.2. Janitorial Services**
Tenant shall directly contract for daily janitorial services regarding the interior of the Premises. Waste must be disposed of in designated areas only and in accordance with the Building’s recycling protocols.

---

## 12. LANDLORD RESPONSIBILITIES

**12.1. Structural Maintenance**
Landlord shall be responsible for the repair and maintenance of the structural portions of the Building, including foundations, exterior walls, load-bearing columns, and roof systems.

**12.2. Building Systems**
Landlord shall operate and maintain the Building’s core mechanical and electrical systems up to the connection point to the Premises, including:

- Passenger and service elevators.
- Central HVAC plant (chillers and cooling towers).
- Main electrical switchgear and risers.
- Fire suppression systems (sprinklers and hydrants) in Common Areas.

---

## 13. ALTERATIONS AND IMPROVEMENTS

**13.1. Approvals Required**
Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements ("**Alterations**") to the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for non-structural work.

For all Alterations, Tenant must submit:

- Detailed architectural and engineering plans ("**Design Drawings**");
- Names and credentials of proposed contractors;
- Construction schedule and risk assessment.

**13.2. Fit-Out Guidelines**
All construction work must comply with the Building Fit-Out Guidelines and generally accepted engineering practices. Tenant is responsible for obtaining all necessary government permits (e.g., Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung or equivalent) for its interior layout.

**13.3. Restoration**
Upon expiration or termination of this Lease, Tenant shall remove all trade fixtures, furniture, and personal property. Tenant shall restore the Premises to its original "bare shell" condition or "fitted" condition (as mutually agreed in the Handover Protocol), repairing any damage caused by such removal.

---

## 14. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

**14.1. Electricity**
Electricity consumption within the Premises shall be separately metered. Tenant shall pay for electricity usage based on the applicable tariff charged by the State Electricity Company (PLN) plus a reasonable administrative surcharge (not exceeding 5%) to cover line losses and infrastructure maintenance.

**14.2. Air Conditioning (HVAC)**
Standard air conditioning hours are:

- **Monday – Friday:** 08:00 to 18:00
- **Saturday:** 08:00 to 13:00
- **Sunday & Public Holidays:** Off

**14.3. Overtime HVAC**
If Tenant requires air conditioning outside Standard Hours ("**Overtime HVAC**"), Tenant must request such usage in writing at least 24 hours in advance. Overtime HVAC shall be charged at the prevailing hourly rate established by Landlord (currently IDR 450,000 per hour per zone).

**14.4. Telecommunications**
Tenant may contract directly with approved internet and telecommunication service providers available in the Building.

---

## 15. ACCESS AND INSPECTION

**15.1. Landlord’s Right of Entry**
Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises during business hours upon giving at least **24 hours’ prior written notice** to inspect the Premises, make necessary repairs, or show the Premises to prospective lenders or purchasers.

**15.2. Emergency Access**
In the event of an emergency (fire, flood, or immediate danger to life/property), Landlord shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time without prior notice. Landlord shall notify Tenant of such entry as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter.

**15.3. Tenant’s 24/7 Access**
Tenant and its authorized employees shall have access to the Premises 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, subject to security identification protocols (e.g., access cards).
