## 2. PREMISES

**2.1. Description of Leased Premises**
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the commercial office space described as follows (the "**Premises**"):

- **Location:** Graha Sentosa Tower (the "Building")
- **Floors:** 5, 6, and 7 (entire floor plates)
- **Address:** Jl. Asia Afrika No. 88, Bandung 40112, Indonesia
- **Measurement:** Approximately **1,500 square meters** (semigross), measured in accordance with BOMA (Building Owners and Managers Association) International standards.
- **Parking:** Right to use twenty (20) unreserved parking spaces in the Building’s parking garage, subject to the Parking Rules and Regulations.

**2.2. Condition of Premises**
The Premises are leased in an "As-Is, Where-Is" condition, as partially fitted out. A detailed Inventory of Fixtures and Fittings is attached to this Agreement as **Schedule A**. Tenant acknowledges inspecting the Premises and accepting its current state of repair.

## 3. LEASE TERM

**3.1. Initial Term**
The term of this lease shall be for a period of **seven (7) years** (the "**Term**").

- **Commencement Date:** **1 April 2025**
- **Expiration Date:** **31 March 2032**

**3.2. Handover Date**
Possession of the Premises shall be tendered to Tenant on **25 March 2025** (the "**Handover Date**") for the purpose of Tenant’s specialized fit-out works. This period between the Handover Date and Commencement Date shall be rent-free (`Fit-Out Period`), provided Tenant does not conduct commercial business operations during this time.

**3.3. Renewal Option**
Tenant shall have the option to renew this Agreement for one (1) additional term of **five (5) years** (the "**Renewal Term**"), subject to:
- Tenant providing written notice of intent to renew no later than six (6) months prior to the Expiration Date;
- Tenant not being in material default of any provision of this Agreement at the time of notice and commencement of the Renewal Term;
- Rent for the Renewal Term being set at the then-prevailing Market Rate, but in no event less than the Base Rent of the final year of the initial Term.

---

## 4. INTENDED USE

**4.1. Permitted Use**
Tenant shall use the Premises strictly for the following purposes ("**Permitted Use**"):
- General executive and administrative corporate offices.
- Staff training and development facilities.
- Ancillary business support functions (e.g., server rooms, pantry for staff, internal meeting rooms).

**4.2. Prohibited Use**
Tenant shall not use, or permit the Premises to be used, for:
- Retail sales to the general public involving high foot traffic.
- Manufacturing or industrial processing.
- Storage of hazardous materials (except standard office cleaning supplies).
- Any illegal or immoral activities.
- Residential or sleeping purposes.

---

## 5. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**5.1. Definitions**
For purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:

- **"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.
- **"Base Rent"** refers to the fixed rental fee payable by Tenant pursuant to Clause 6, exclusive of Service Charges, taxes, and other costs.
- **"Building Rules"** means the regulations governing the operation and use of the Building as established and amended by Landlord from time to time.
- **"Common Areas"** means those areas of the Building intended for general use by all tenants, including lobbies, elevators, stairwells, and public restrooms.
- **"Force Majeure"** has the meaning set forth in Clause 19.
- **"Operating Expenses"** or **"Service Charge"** means the costs incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining, and repairing the Building and Common Areas.
- **"Laws"** means all applicable laws, statutes, regulations, ordinances, and decrees of the Republic of Indonesia and the local government of Bandung.
