## 4. Historical Context

- Iran’s nuclear program started under US "Atoms for Peace" program (1967).
- After 1979 Islamic Revolution, US embargoes and sanctions began; 2015 JCPOA limited enriching uranium to 3.67% and froze nuclear activities in exchange for sanctions relief.
- US withdrew in 2018 under Trump, reimposing sanctions; Iran then escalated enrichment to 60%.
- Proxy conflicts with US allies, assassinations (e.g., Qassem Soleimani 2020), cyberattacks, and regional wars have intensified.
- Prior US-Iran negotiations repeatedly failed to produce durable settlements.
