EVERGREEN CORPORATION — Q3 2026 STRATEGIC REVIEW
CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY

Prepared by: Strategic Planning Office
Date: 12 October 2026
Distribution: Executive Committee, Regional Directors

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The third quarter of 2026 delivered mixed results against the company's
five-year growth plan. Revenue grew 8.4 percent year-over-year, but this fell
short of the 12 percent target established in the January board meeting. The
shortfall was concentrated in the Asia-Pacific region, where the launch of the
Solara product line was delayed by regulatory review and supply-chain
constraints in the Vietnam manufacturing hub.

Profit margins improved by 1.2 percentage points due to the cost reduction
initiative launched in Q1. The initiative, internally codenamed "Project
Thrift," consolidated vendor contracts and reduced logistics overhead by 14
percent. However, several department heads have raised concerns that the cuts
have strained the quality assurance team, which reported a 22 percent increase
in post-launch defect tickets during the quarter.

KEY INITIATIVES

Project Thrift: The procurement team renegotiated contracts with seventeen
critical suppliers. The largest savings came from the packaging division, where
a switch to regional suppliers reduced per-unit costs by 9 percent. The
initiative is on track to exceed its annual savings target of 47 million
dollars.

Solara Launch: The Solara line was originally scheduled for a July release in
the Asia-Pacific region. Regulatory approval is now expected in mid-November,
and the company has shifted the global launch window to Q1 2027. Marketing has
been asked to revise the campaign timeline and preserve the pre-order pipeline.

Riverstone Acquisition: The acquisition of Riverstone Analytics closed on 22
August. Integration is progressing, but the migration of customer data to the
Evergreen data lake has been slower than planned. The Chief Information Officer
has flagged a three-week delay in the unified analytics dashboard.

RISKS AND CONCERNS

The quality assurance team has requested three additional headcount to manage
the increased defect volume. The Chief Operating Officer has deferred the
request until Q4 pending a review of whether the ticket increase is a temporary
anomaly or a structural problem.

Competitive pressure in the European market intensified after competitor
AuroraTech introduced a comparable product at a 15 percent price discount.
Evergreen's market research team is conducting a rapid pricing study to
determine whether to match the discount or emphasize the premium feature set.

NEXT STEPS

The executive committee will meet on 28 October to finalize the Q4 budget
reallocation and approve the revised Solara launch plan. Regional directors are
asked to submit updated forecasts by 22 October.
