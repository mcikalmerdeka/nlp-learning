CHARTER OF THE ASTERIA COLONY

Adopted by the Founding Council on 7 July 2389
Ratified by the colonists on 21 July 2389

PREAMBLE

We, the founders of the Asteria Colony on the moon of Prometheus IV, establish
this Charter to secure justice, cooperation, and sustainability for all
residents of the colony. We recognize that survival on this world depends on
shared stewardship of air, water, soil, and knowledge. This Charter shall be the
supreme law of the colony and may be amended only by a two-thirds vote of the
General Assembly.

ARTICLE I — GOVERNANCE

The colony shall be governed by a General Assembly composed of all adult
residents who have completed at least one standard year of residence. The
General Assembly shall elect a seven-person Council annually. The Council shall
manage day-to-day administration, allocate public resources, and enforce the
Charter.

No Council member may serve more than three consecutive terms. No resident may
hold more than one Council seat at a time. Decisions of the Council require a
majority vote, except where this Charter requires a supermajority.

ARTICLE II — RESOURCE STEWARDSHIP

Air, water, and soil are the common heritage of the colony and shall not be
privately owned. The Council shall set quotas for personal and agricultural
water use, and any resident exceeding twice the standard quota without Council
approval shall face a graduated penalty.

The colony's fusion reactor, greenhouses, and atmospheric processors are
public infrastructure. Maintenance schedules and operational data shall be
published to all residents within forty-eight hours of any incident.

ARTICLE III — KNOWLEDGE AND EDUCATION

All scientific, medical, and technical knowledge generated using colony
resources shall be archived in the Public Library and made freely available to
residents. The Council may impose a temporary embargo of no more than two years
on information that poses an immediate security or health risk.

Every resident between the ages of six and eighteen shall receive education in
life-support systems, agriculture, engineering, and colonial governance.

ARTICLE IV — JUSTICE AND RIGHTS

Residents shall enjoy freedom of expression, assembly, and movement within the
colony, provided such activities do not endanger life-support systems.

Disputes between residents shall first be mediated by a neutral arbitrator
appointed by the Council. Appeals may be brought before the General Assembly.

No resident shall be exiled from the colony except following conviction of a
crime that endangers the survival of the colony, and then only by a two-thirds
vote of the General Assembly.

ARTICLE V — AMENDMENT

This Charter may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the General Assembly, with at
least sixty days' notice of the proposed amendment. Amendments that alter the
resource-stewardship provisions of Article II require a three-fourths vote.

Founding Council of Asteria Colony
Prometheus IV, 2389
